# Countdown

How many days until the moments that matter: a trip, a deadline, and birthdays or anniversaries that
come back every year. Offline, no key.

## What it does

- `countdown` - every day you keep, the nearest first
- `countdown add "<name>" <date>` - `YYYY-MM-DD` (or `20 Dec 2026`) once, `MM-DD` (or `14 March`) every year
- `countdown remove "<name>"`
- `countdown until <date>` - days to any date, and how many of them are working days

## Setup

Your days are also listed under Integrations, one per line as `Name|date`.

## Say to Iris

- "How long until our holiday?"
- "Remember Anna's birthday on 14 March."
- "How many working days until 20 December?"
