# Holidays

Public holidays at home and in more than a hundred countries, through
[Nager.Date](https://date.nager.at). No key and no account.

## What it does

- `holidays` - the next public holidays at home
- `holidays next <country>` - somewhere else
- `holidays year [year] [country]` - a whole year
- `holidays today [country]` - is today a public holiday
- `holidays long [year] [country]` - long weekends, and which day to take off to make one
- `holidays countries` - the countries that are known

## Setup

Set your country under Integrations (NL by default), or say "my country is Belgium".

## Say to Iris

- "When is the next public holiday?"
- "Is Monday a holiday in Germany?"
- "Which long weekends are left this year?"

## Permissions

`internet`: to ask Nager.Date. Only the country code and the year are sent.
