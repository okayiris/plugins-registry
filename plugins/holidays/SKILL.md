---
name: holidays
description: Public holidays in more than a hundred countries through the holidays plugin (Nager.Date, free, no key). The next ones, a whole year, whether today is one, and long weekends with the bridge days that make one.
whenToUse: When the owner asks when the next public holiday is, whether a day is a holiday, what the holidays are this year somewhere, or when to take a day off for a long weekend. Not for school holidays or the owner's own days off.
---

# holidays

```sh
holidays                     # the next public holidays at home
holidays next Germany        # somewhere else, by name or code
holidays year 2027 BE
holidays today
holidays long                # long weekends this year, with the day off to take
holidays settings set country NL
```

- Regional holidays say where they apply ("only in BY, BW"). Say that part out loud.
- School holidays are not public holidays; this plugin does not know them.
