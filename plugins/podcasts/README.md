# Podcasts

Find podcasts, follow them, and hear what is new this week. Shows are found in the Apple
Podcasts directory; episodes come straight from each show's own feed, each with its length and audio link.
No key and no account.

## What it does

- `podcasts` - new episodes of the shows you follow, the last seven days
- `podcasts search <words>` - shows that fit
- `podcasts follow <number or name>`, `podcasts unfollow <name>`
- `podcasts episodes <name> [count]` - the latest of one show
- `podcasts play <number>` - the summary and audio link of an episode
- `podcasts list` - the shows you follow

## Say to Iris

- "Find a podcast about history."
- "Follow De Dag."
- "Anything new in my podcasts?"

## Permissions

`internet`: to search the directory and read the feeds of the shows you follow.
