# Recipes

Recipes for tonight from [TheMealDB](https://www.themealdb.com): search by dish, by what is in the
fridge, or by cuisine. Every recipe comes with its ingredients and amounts, the steps, and a single
grocery line to put on your shopping list. No key and no account.

## What it does

- `recipes <dish>` - recipes whose name fits
- `recipes with <ingredient>` - recipes with what you have
- `recipes from <cuisine>` - Italian, Indian, Mexican...
- `recipes show <number or name>` - ingredients and steps
- `recipes groceries <number or name>` - only the ingredients, one line
- `recipes random` - a surprise

## Say to Iris

- "Show me an Italian recipe."

## Permissions

`internet`: to ask TheMealDB. Only the search words are sent.
