# sun

```sh
sun                         # today at home
sun Lisbon
sun on 21 Dec
sun on 2026-06-21 Tromso
sun moon
sun settings set place Utrecht
```

- Near the poles the sun may not rise or set at all; the plugin says so.
- Times are within a minute or two; say "around" for the moon's phases.
