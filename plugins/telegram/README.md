## What it does

Talk to Iris on Telegram, from anywhere, also when her page is closed. Type or send a voice message; she
answers in the same chat. When she has something for you on her own, a reminder or a job that is done, and
the page is not open, it arrives on Telegram.

- "Remind me at five to call the garage."
- "What's on my calendar tomorrow?"
- A voice message on the way home: "Put milk and eggs on the list."

## Linking

Ask Iris to connect Telegram, or type /telegram. She gives you a link; open it where you have Telegram and
tap Start. That's it. The link works once, for an hour.

Or do it yourself in your Iris, under Integrations: telegram, set up, and switch Connected on or off.

## Good to know

- Iris talks through the official bot @OkayIrisBot. Only your own chat is linked to your Iris; anyone else
  who messages the bot gets nothing from her.
- Group chats are not supported: in a group everyone could talk to your assistant.
- Unlink any time: ask Iris, or send /stop to the bot.
