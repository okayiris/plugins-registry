# TV

What is on TV tonight, and when the next episode of the shows you follow comes out, from
[TVmaze](https://www.tvmaze.com). No key and no account.

## What it does

- `tv` - the shows you follow: episodes in the next two weeks
- `tv tonight [country]` - prime time tonight, per channel (NL by default)
- `tv show <name>` - status, channel, the last and the next episode
- `tv follow <name>`, `tv unfollow <name>`

## Say to Iris

- "What's on TV tonight?"
- "When is the next episode of Wie is de Mol?"
- "Follow The Last of Us."

## Permissions

`internet`: to ask TVmaze. Only the show names and the country are sent.
