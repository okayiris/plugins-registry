---
name: tv
description: Television and series through the tv plugin (TVmaze, no key): prime time tonight per channel, one show's status with its last and next episode, and the owner's followed shows with what comes in the next two weeks.
whenToUse: When the owner asks what is on TV tonight, when a new episode or season of a show comes, whether a show has ended, or wants a show followed.
---

# tv

```sh
tv                          # followed shows: the next two weeks
tv tonight                  # prime time, NL by default; tv tonight BE
tv show wie is de mol       # one show
tv follow the bear
tv unfollow the bear
```

- Streaming shows (Netflix, Disney+) have their episodes too, but not in the channel listing of tonight.
- Say the day and time of an episode in the owner's words: "Thursday evening at eight".
