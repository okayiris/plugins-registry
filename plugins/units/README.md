# Units

Convert units the way you would say it: "5 miles in km", "350 F to C", "2 cups in ml". Offline and
exact, no internet and no key.

## What it does

- `units <amount> <unit> [to] <unit>` - convert
- `units <amount> <unit>` - to the usual unit on the other side (metric or imperial)
- `units list` - every unit that is known

Length, weight, volume and US cooking measures (tsp, tbsp, cup), temperature, speed, area, data sizes
(kB and KiB), time, and fuel use (l/100km and mpg).

## Say to Iris

- "How many kilometres is a half marathon in miles?"
- "The recipe says 350 Fahrenheit, what is that in Celsius?"
- "How much is 180 pounds in kilos?"
