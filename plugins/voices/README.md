## What it does

Iris speaks with a voice of her own, and you choose which one. Type /voices, or just ask her to sound
different: calmer, deeper, a man's voice.

- Iris: 2 voices, a woman's and a man's.
- Plus: 20 voices.
- Max: 200 voices.

On the free plan Iris speaks with your phone's or browser's own voice.

Hear a voice before you choose: ask her to let you hear one ("let me hear a deep British voice"), and it plays
on the page. You can listen to the voices of a bigger plan too.

## Good to know

- Every voice is synthetic, made for Iris. None is a copy of a real person's voice.
- She switches from her next sentence on. Don't like it? Ask for another one, or /voices.
- Change plans and a voice your new plan doesn't have is set aside, not lost: she speaks with a similar voice
  until the plan has it again.
