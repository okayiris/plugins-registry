# Wikipedia

Look anything up on **Wikipedia**, in your own language. No key and no account.

## What it does

- `wikipedia <subject>` - the short summary of the best fitting article, with its link
- `wikipedia more <subject>` - the whole opening of the article
- `wikipedia search <text>` - the articles that match
- `wikipedia today` - what happened on this day in history
- `wikipedia random` - a random article

## Setup

Choose the Wikipedia to read under Integrations (`en` by default, `nl`, `de`, `fr` and so on).
