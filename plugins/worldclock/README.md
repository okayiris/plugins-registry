# Worldclock

The time anywhere in the world, what your time is somewhere else, and when a call fits in everyone's
working hours. Works offline from the time zone database: no key, no internet.

## What it does

- `worldclock` - the time now in the places you keep
- `worldclock <place>` - the time now in one place, and how far ahead or behind it is
- `worldclock at <time> [in <place>]` - that time in your places
- `worldclock meet [place, place]` - the hours that are between 9 and 17 everywhere
- `worldclock add <place>`, `worldclock remove <place>`

## Setup

Your places are listed under Integrations (London, New York and Tokyo to start with).

## Say to Iris

- "What time is it in Sydney?"
- "If I call at four, what time is that in San Francisco?"
- "When can I plan a call with London and New York?"
